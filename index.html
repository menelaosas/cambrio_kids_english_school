Our English Programs

Tailored programmes for every age and level — from first words to exam success.

child_care
Ages 4–6

Kindergarten English

A fun and gentle introduction to English for young children through songs, games, stories, movement, and classroom routines. Students develop basic vocabulary, listening skills, pronunciation, confidence, and a positive first experience with English.

auto_stories
Primary Age

Young Learners English

A structured programme for primary-age students to build strong English foundations. Students develop vocabulary, grammar, phonics, reading, writing, listening, and speaking skills through engaging and age-appropriate activities.

workspace_premium
A1 – A2

Cambridge Preparation for Kids

A focused programme that prepares young learners for Cambridge English levels from A1 to A2. Students practise exam-style listening, speaking, reading, and writing tasks while improving accuracy, confidence, and test-taking skills.

school
B1 – B2

Cambridge Preparation for Teenagers

A Cambridge-focused course for teenage students who want to improve their academic English and prepare for B1 to B2 exams. Students develop stronger grammar, vocabulary, reading comprehension, essay writing, speaking fluency, and listening skills.

record_voice_over
All Ages

Communication English Classes

Practical English classes designed to help students use English naturally in real-life situations. Students improve speaking fluency, pronunciation, listening comprehension, confidence, conversation skills, and everyday communication.

person_pin
Online & Offline

Private English Lessons

Personalised one-on-one or small-group lessons based on each student's level, goals, and learning needs. Students receive individual support to improve speaking, grammar, pronunciation, writing, exam preparation, or school English.
